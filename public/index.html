Shop Cart Staff

A small shop, built to break on purpose.

Six watches, real stock, a simulated payment provider and a webhook that sometimes arrives twice. This is a learning project about the parts of e-commerce that are genuinely hard: holding stock, database transactions, duplicate events, and two people buying the last item at the same moment.
